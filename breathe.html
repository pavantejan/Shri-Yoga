Take a breath

Box breathing · 4 seconds in, 4 hold, 4 out. One minute of calm, right here.

Ready?

Breathe with the circle. Tap the button to pause anytime.

How it works

  1. Breathe in slowly through the nose for 4 counts.
  2. Hold gently for 4 counts, without strain.
  3. Breathe out slowly through the mouth for 4 counts.
